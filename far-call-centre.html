What do the controls do?
Screen
Talk privately with a show caller to check what they want to discuss.
Ready
Mark a screened caller as ready. They still cannot be heard by listeners.
Put on air
Connect a ready caller to the programme. You must confirm first.
Hold
Take the caller off air or out of screening and let them wait.
Mute
Stop listeners hearing the caller without ending their connection.
End call
Disconnect the caller. They must call again to return.
