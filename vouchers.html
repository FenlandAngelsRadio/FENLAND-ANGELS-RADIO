HOW IT WILL WORK

Local offers. Clear terms.

  1. Choose a local offer

    See which business accepts the voucher, what it includes, its price, expiry date and any booking or spending conditions.

  2. Buy through SumUp

    Once sales open, checkout will be handled by SumUp. Each offer will explain how and when you will receive your voucher.

  3. Redeem with the business

    Follow the offer's redemption instructions and present your voucher to the named business before it expires.

Can I buy a voucher now?

Not yet. We are preparing the first offers. There are currently no voucher products or payment links on this page.

Where can I use a voucher?

Each voucher will be valid only at the business named on that offer, subject to its published terms. It will not be a general balance to spend at any business.

What will I need to check before buying?

The entitlement, selling price, expiry date, delivery method, booking requirements and any restrictions. Offer-specific terms and the applicable purchase and refund information will be available before checkout opens.

How does my business join?

Send a voucher exchange enquiry with your proposed offer and quantity. FAR will agree the voucher value, redemption conditions and existing non-audio advertising package with you before either side commits.
