## Automatic pricing

Advertising credit = actual voucher face value × agreed quantity.

Selling price = 50% of face value rounded to the nearest £1, with exactly 50p rounding up.

| Actual face value | Rounded value for pricing | Customer pays |
|---|---|---|
| £20.49 | £20 | £10 |
| £20.50 | £21 | £10.50 |
| £20.75 | £21 | £10.50 |

The calculation is enforced by the database as well as displayed automatically in the form. An agreement cannot import more than its agreed quantity or begin selling before the full quantity is loaded. No real vouchers or agreements have been added.
