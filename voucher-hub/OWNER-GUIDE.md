# FAR Voucher Hub — owner and team guide

Open the FAR website admin dashboard and choose **Commercial → Voucher Hub**. Direct address: https://fenlandangelsradio.co.uk/voucher-app.html.

## Give your team access

In **Staff & Permissions**, management can choose **Voucher Hub** when inviting someone. For an existing staff member, tick **Voucher Hub** and save permissions. Owners and deputy managers already have management access. This does not give businesses station access.

## Add the first business and offer

1. In the hub, choose **Businesses** and save its name and contact email.
2. Choose **Offers & stock → New offer**. Enter the voucher's real face value, quantity, expiry and terms. Add the existing non-audio advertising package, start/end dates and delivery commitment.
3. The app calculates advertising credit at £1 per £1 of actual face value supplied. It rounds each voucher's face value to the nearest £1 (50p rounds up), then halves that rounded figure for the customer price.
4. Save the offer and choose **Create agreement draft**. Complete and review the wording with the business, obtain signatures outside the app, then upload the signed agreement and original voucher file.
5. Add the agreed quantity, or paste the business's original codes. FAR codes are generated automatically. Listing requires the complete agreed quantity. Listing an offer does not switch on payments.

## Invite the business

Under **Businesses → Approve a business login**, select the business and enter its work email. **Approve & invite** sends its invitation. The business opens the same hub link, signs in and sees only its own voucher redemption screen. It can choose **Add to phone** for installation or home-screen instructions.

At the counter, scan a QR code or type the FAR code, choose **Check voucher**, then **Confirm redemption**. A code is consumed once. If the connection fails, check again before accepting it. Paused, expired and already-redeemed vouchers must not be accepted.

## Daily operation

**Overview** shows stock totals, advertising dates and commitments. The dates help the team track delivery; the app does not publish adverts for you. **Connections** shows the last successful background-processing run and tasks needing attention. Payment verification, voucher email delivery and configured accounting tasks are handled automatically.

**Export register** downloads the voucher register. **Export agreements** downloads one row per offer, with non-cash advertising credit, quantity, dates and commitments. **Sales & reviews → Export sales** downloads order amounts, SumUp fees, payment dates and review statuses for reconciliation. These are private staff downloads.

## A customer issue or refund

Management uses **Sales & reviews → Pause voucher** to stop redemption while reviewing an issue. This does not refund money. Make any agreed refund through the owner's SumUp account, then choose **Check payment** in the hub. A provider-confirmed refund or reversal keeps the voucher blocked and requires the original sale/refund to be reconciled in accounting. It never adds stock back for resale.

If no refund is made, **Release pause** first verifies that SumUp still confirms a valid payment. Previously redeemed vouchers remain redeemed. The app records who paused or released a voucher.
