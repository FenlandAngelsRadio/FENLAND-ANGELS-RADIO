Fenland Angels Radio – The Sound of the Fens

Welcome to Fenland Angels Radio, an independent music radio community serving listeners across the wider Fens.

Fenland Angels Radio broadcasts online 24 hours a day, bringing together music, local information, community events, independent artists and original programmes from across Fenland, West Norfolk, South Lincolnshire and the surrounding Fen communities.

Our aim is to provide an accessible local radio service that reflects the people, places, businesses, artists and communities across the Fens. Alongside our music programming, FAR provides local news, weather, traffic and travel information, community features, events information and opportunities for local organisations and businesses to reach listeners.

NEWS – Fenland Angels Radio provides local and wider news updates throughout the day. Our aim is to provide clear, accurate and useful information relevant to listeners across the Fens. News content is also published through the FAR website.

WEATHER – Regular weather information is provided for the wider Fens area, including communities across West Norfolk, Fenland, parts of Cambridgeshire and South Lincolnshire.

COMMUNITY – FAR supports local communities by promoting community organisations, charities, local initiatives, independent businesses, events and activities. We welcome information from organisations and individuals across the Fens who have something relevant to share with our listeners.

LOCAL EVENTS – Our What’s On Across the Fens service promotes events, activities, festivals, community meetings, markets, entertainment and other local happenings. Event organisers can submit events directly through the FAR website for review before publication.

TRAFFIC AND TRAVEL – Traffic and travel information is included within relevant FAR programming, particularly during Breakfast and Drive Time. Updates focus on useful information affecting journeys across the station’s wider coverage area.

COMPETITIONS – Fenland Angels Radio may occasionally run competitions, quizzes and listener features within programmes or through our website and social media channels. Any specific rules or closing dates will be made clear when a competition is promoted.

MUSIC – Fenland Angels Radio broadcasts a broad mixture of music alongside a strong commitment to independent and emerging artists. Programming varies throughout the day, with specialist programmes and themed music alongside FAR’s general station output.

We aim to give independent artists opportunities to have their music heard and provide listeners with music they may not hear through larger commercial radio services.

PODCASTS – Interviews, features, programme highlights and other listen-again material may be made available through the FAR Podcasts section of the website.

LISTEN – Fenland Angels Radio broadcasts online 24 hours a day. Listeners can use the Listen Live player on the FAR website and other listening methods listed through our Other Ways to Listen page.

BE PART OF FENLAND ANGELS RADIO – FAR welcomes applications from people interested in becoming presenters, DJs, producers, members of the road team, news or weather contributors, social media volunteers, technical team members and other station volunteers.

Applications can be submitted through the Presenter / DJ / Staff Applications page on the FAR website.

SCHEDULE – Our current programme schedule is available on the FAR website. The schedule includes live and automated programming, specialist shows, Breakfast, Drive Time and weekend programming. Programme times may occasionally change for special broadcasts or operational reasons.

PRESENTERS AND DJs – FAR programmes are presented by members of the station team and contributing DJs. Some programmes may be produced or presented remotely while forming part of the main Fenland Angels Radio schedule.

ADVERTISING AND SPONSORSHIP – Fenland Angels Radio offers advertising and sponsorship opportunities for businesses, organisations and community groups. Current packages and enquiry information are available through the Advertising & Sponsorship section of the website.

Advertising and sponsorship arrangements are kept separate from FAR’s editorial decisions and news coverage.

VOUCHERS – FAR may provide voucher offers and promotions through the FAR Voucher Shop. Availability, prices, terms and redemption information will be published on the website when relevant offers are available.

CONTACTING FAR – General enquiries can be submitted through the Contact Us page on the Fenland Angels Radio website. Listeners can also contact FAR through our official social media channels.

MANAGEMENT – Fenland Angels Radio is operated as part of Bastone Major Group Ltd. Day-to-day station operations, programming, website services and community activity are managed by the FAR station team.

OWNERSHIP – The Fenland Angels Radio name, branding, website and associated station assets are operated by Bastone Major Group Ltd.

COMPLAINTS AND FEEDBACK – Fenland Angels Radio welcomes constructive feedback from listeners.

If you have a complaint about something broadcast, published or provided by Fenland Angels Radio, please contact us through the Contact Us page and include as much information as possible, including the programme, article, date and approximate time where relevant.

We will review complaints fairly and aim to respond within a reasonable period. Where a correction is appropriate, FAR will take reasonable steps to correct or clarify the relevant information.
