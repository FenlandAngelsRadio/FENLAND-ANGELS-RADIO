Clear terms before you book

Each production bundle covers one initial advert and one month of airtime. Weekly spots are scheduled across the agreed time bands; we confirm dates and the total number of spots in your written quote. A spot is one broadcast of your advert, not one listener.

The production brief, script support, voice, music, revisions, delivery date, usage rights and any applicable tax are confirmed before payment. Your campaign starts after you approve the finished advert. Renewal airtime and any new production are quoted separately, so you do not automatically pay for a new advert every month.

Website and social placements run during the campaign. We provide a summary of scheduled/broadcast spots and digital placements; listener figures are supplied only where measured and available. Advertising does not guarantee listener numbers, enquiries or sales. Independent editorial interviews are arranged separately from paid advertising.
