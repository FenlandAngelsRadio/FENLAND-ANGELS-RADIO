Ask about advertising, sponsorship or a campaign that does not fit a standard package.

Offer vouchers in exchange for advertising

Tell us what each voucher includes, its face value, how many you can offer, the expiry date and any redemption restrictions. If you have an existing non-audio FAR package in mind, include its name.

You receive £1 of advertising for each £1 of vouchers supplied at actual face value. FAR rounds each voucher’s face value to the nearest pound (50p rounds up), sells it for half that amount and retains the proceeds. Sending this enquiry does not commit you to an exchange. We will agree the advertising package and voucher terms with you first.
