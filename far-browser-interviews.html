Browser interviews

Checking staff access…

Open your guest controls first, then invite a guest by sending this private link. They only need a browser, a microphone and headphones.

Choose your presenter microphone in the guest controls so guests can hear you. Use headphones and keep the public radio player closed. Your presenter microphone still goes to PlayIt through Remote Studio.

Sending guest audio into PlayIt

The station PC receives Scene 1 through its installed VB-Audio cable. Guests stay out of Scene 1 until you add them using the guest controls. Joining the room alone does not put them on air.

On the station PC, open the receiver below. In PlayIt, use an Aux Input with input CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable), title FAR Browser Interviews, and the interview duration. It must be playing for Scene 1 to reach the broadcast. Stop the Aux Input when the interview ends.

Use S1 in the guest controls to add/remove a guest from the feed. Guest mic mute and volume are there too. Screen guests before adding them. Browser interviews currently have no verified Dump protection.

Receiver output must be CABLE Input (VB-Audio Virtual Cable), never the default speakers or the presenter microphone. This receiver is only for the station PC. Keep it open throughout the interview.
